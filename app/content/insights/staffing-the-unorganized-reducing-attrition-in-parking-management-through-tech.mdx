## The Talent Crisis in Parking

One of the biggest headaches for any parking vendor in India isn't the cars—it’s the people. The parking industry suffers from some of the highest labor turnover (attrition) rates. Finding reliable staff who can handle manual billing, manage frustrated customers, and show up consistently is a constant struggle.

---

## The Pain of High Attrition

### Constant Training
Every time a staff member leaves, you waste valuable time training a new person on manual processes that are prone to error.

### Operational Downtime
If an attendant doesn't show up, your entry gate becomes a bottleneck. This causes immediate traffic jams and long-term loss of customer loyalty.

### Dependence on Unskilled Labor
Manual systems force staff to act as "calculators" and "security guards" simultaneously. This high-stress environment leads to quick burnout and frequent exits.

---

## Reducing Dependency through Automation

Prothom AI changes the role of the parking attendant. By implementing our technology, you transition from a "labor-heavy" model to a **"tech-light" model**.

### Self-Serve Tech
With QR-code scans and automated barriers, the customer handles the "work" of entry and exit. This significantly reduces the total headcount required on-site.

### Error-Proofing
Since the software handles the calculations and the payments, your staff doesn't need specialized training. This creates a lower-stress environment where employees are more likely to stay.

### Remote Management
Owners can manage multiple sites with a skeleton crew. The "brain" of the operation resides in the cloud—following a **"Zero Trust" philosophy** where the system validates data rather than relying on a temporary worker's manual input.
